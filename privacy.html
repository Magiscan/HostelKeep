HostelKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31/07/2026

HostelKeep is a hostel cleaning management app that helps managers and cleaners organise daily room cleaning. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: username, email address (synthetic, auto‑generated by the app), and hostel code.
  • Room cleaning data: room assignments, cleaning statuses, notes left by cleaners, and time spent on extra cleaning.
  • Photos: when a cleaner requests an extra room they may attach a photo of the room; these photos are stored temporarily to help the manager decide whether to approve the request.
  • Push notification tokens: if you allow notifications, we store a device token to send you real‑time alerts (e.g., when a room is cleaned or a request is made).

2. How we use your information

All data is used solely for the purpose of operating the HostelKeep service:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Assign rooms to cleaners and track cleaning progress
  • Allow cleaners to request extra rooms and send notes
  • Send push notifications about cleaning events (only to managers of the same hostel)
  • Provide a dashboard for hostel managers

3. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on Firebase Firestore, a secure cloud database provided by Google. All communication between the app and Firebase is encrypted using HTTPS. Access to data is restricted by strict security rules that ensure users can only see information belonging to their own hostel.

Photos attached to extra‑room requests are stored as compressed Base64 strings directly in the database. They are automatically deleted when the request is approved or rejected.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only accessible to members of your own hostel team (managers and cleaners).

5. Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting your hostel manager. If you are a manager, you can delete your entire hostel from within the app.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: open the Account tab and choose Delete account. Cleaners' accounts and personal data are removed immediately. If you are a manager, deleting your account also permanently deletes your hostel, its rooms, its cleaning history and all cleaner accounts belonging to it.

If you cannot access the app, email YOUR_EMAIL and we will delete your account within 30 days.

9. Data retention

Cleaning records and notes are kept for as long as the hostel exists. Notifications are removed automatically after 24 hours. Photos attached to extra-room requests are deleted as soon as the request is approved or rejected. Deleting your account removes your personal data immediately.
