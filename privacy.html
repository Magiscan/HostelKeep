HostelKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20/09/2026

HostelKeep is a hostel housekeeping management app. Hostel managers set up rooms and assign them to housekeepers; housekeepers see the rooms assigned to them and mark them as they clean. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, and what rights you have over it.

When a hostel uses HostelKeep to manage its own staff, that hostel decides who has an account and what is recorded about their work. In those matters the hostel acts as the controller of its employees' data, and we act as its processor.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. Managers and manager-side staff sign in with their own real email address, which is also used for password resets. Housekeepers do not need an email address: their manager creates their account with a username, and the app generates an internal, non-working address for them behind the scenes. We also store the display name, the hostel code, and the role.
  • Profile and hostel photos. Optional. A profile photo, and a logo for the hostel.
  • Room and cleaning data. Rooms, floors and beds, room assignments, cleaning states, notes written by managers and housekeepers, extra time recorded, and extra-room requests.
  • Room photos. When a housekeeper asks for an extra room, they may attach a photo of it so the manager can decide.
  • Daily history. For hostels on a paid plan, a daily snapshot of the work done is stored so managers can review and export it.
  • Push notification tokens. If you allow notifications, a device token so the app can alert you about cleaning events.
  • Subscription and billing details. For paying hostels: the contact details and the payment record kept for accounting. Card details are never seen or stored by us; they are handled by our payment provider.

HostelKeep does not track your location, does not use advertising, and does not profile you.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Inside the app, your data is only visible to members of your own hostel. We rely on these providers to run the service:

5. How your data is stored and protected

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Traffic between the app and the database is encrypted with HTTPS. Access is enforced by server-side security rules: an account can only read and write data belonging to its own hostel, and housekeepers can only change the rooms assigned to them. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as the account exists.
  • Rooms and cleaning history: for as long as the hostel exists.
  • Photos attached to extra-room requests: deleted as soon as the request is approved or rejected, and in any case when the request is removed.
  • Notifications: kept until a manager deletes them.
  • Invoices and payment records: kept for as long as Italian tax law requires, currently ten years.

7. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account from inside the app: open Account and choose Delete account.

  • Housekeepers and manager-side staff: your account and personal data are removed immediately. The hostel itself is unaffected.
  • Owners: deleting your account also deletes your hostels, their rooms, all cleaning history, and every housekeeper and staff account belonging to them. An owner with more than one property can instead delete a single hostel and keep the account.

If you cannot access the app, email kohciaostudio@gmail.com from the address on your account, or with your hostel code and username, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Payment records that we must keep by law are retained after deletion, in line with section 6.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or hand it over in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to kohciaostudio@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

If you are an employee of a hostel, requests about your work records may need to be handled by your employer, and we will pass them on.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Children

HostelKeep is a workplace tool for hostel staff. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.
